About Our Company

Glossey exports is an established since 2005. One of the Leading knitted and woven garments manufacturer and exporter in Tirupur, and also offer professional sourcing services for Apparel importers and brand owners across the world. Glossey exports is expertise in cotton/woven knitwear such as T-Shirts, Home Wear for Men, Women and Kids. We are having OEKO TEX certification provided by International Association for Research and Testing in the Field of Textile and Leather Ecology.

Achievement story

We have empaneled over 30 manufacturers who have worked with us to improve their efficiencies and quality processes. We have trained their people and oriented them for best practices. We also continue to look at new manufacturers who could offer great value and ideas.

Prior to working with a manufacturer, we ensure that they demonstrate:

  1. A commitment to build a business with a long term view, by providing value and being ethical
  2. Technical expertise to produce goods as per the specifications defined
  3. A good understanding of the dynamics and expectations of the markets
  4. Financial capacity to support the business
  5. An open mind to learn continuously and to accept changes when appropriate
